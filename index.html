JHS Groove

JHS is a jazz-groove band of five stray cats from the album Urban Heritage. This site holds the policies for sns_automate, the app the band uses to post its own content to its own Instagram, Facebook, Threads and YouTube accounts.

The app is not offered to the public. It acts only on accounts owned by JHS Groove.
