Data Deletion Instructions

App: sns_automate (Meta App ID 877853898272289)

sns_automate stores data only about JHS Groove's own accounts. If you connected an account to this app and want the data removed:

  1. Remove the app: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → sns_automate → Remove. This revokes the app's access right away.
  2. Email jh.shin7274@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your account name or ID.

We delete the stored tokens, account details and post records within 30 days and reply by email when it is done.
