Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-07 · App: sns_automate (Meta App ID 877853898272289)

Who we are

sns_automate is an internal publishing tool operated by JHS Groove (Jaehyun Shin). It posts the band's own photos, videos and captions to the band's own social accounts and reads the performance numbers of those posts.

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens for the JHS Groove Facebook Page, Instagram business account (@jhsgroove), Threads account and YouTube channel.
  • Basic information of those accounts (ID, name, username).
  • Insights for our own posts: likes, comments, views and reach.

The app does not collect data about other people. It does not read private messages, does not build profiles of followers, and does not offer Facebook Login to anyone outside JHS Groove.

How we use it

Only to publish our own content and to measure how it performs. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Tokens and post records are kept in a private database (Supabase) protected by access rules. Tokens never appear on any public page.

How long we keep it

Performance numbers are kept while the account is active. Tokens are replaced when refreshed and deleted when an account is disconnected.

Deleting your data

See Data Deletion. Requests are handled within 30 days.
